# Privacy Policy

**Cambly Transcript Scraper** does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

- All transcript data is extracted locally from the Cambly lesson replay page you have open in your browser.
- Extracted transcripts are saved as JSON files to your local file system only.
- The extension stores your preferred download folder path locally using Chrome's storage API.
- No data is sent to any external server, third-party service, or analytics platform.
- No cookies, tracking pixels, or telemetry of any kind are used.

If you have questions about this policy, please open an issue at [github.com/RealZST/cambly-skill](https://github.com/RealZST/cambly-skill/issues).
